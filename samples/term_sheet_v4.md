# TERM SHEET v4 — SERIES C PREFERRED STOCK & STRATEGIC MERGER COVENANTS

ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL INTERPRETATION

"Churn" means the percentage of Ending ARR lost during a given fiscal quarter due to customer terminations, non-renewals, or voluntary contractions, net of upsells within the same customer cohort.

"ARR" means the annualized recurring value of all active subscription contracts in effect on the final day of the applicable fiscal period, excluding one-time professional services and implementation fees.

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, calculated in accordance with GAAP consistently applied across all prior reporting periods.

"Material Adverse Effect" means any change, event, or occurrence that, individually or in the aggregate, has a materially adverse impact on the business, assets, financial condition, or results of operations of the Company.

"Escrow Account" means the segregated interest-bearing account established with the Escrow Agent at Closing to secure the indemnification obligations of the Selling Stockholders under Article VIII.

"Liquidation Preference" means the senior distribution right of the holders of Series C Preferred Stock upon any voluntary or involuntary liquidation, dissolution, or Deemed Liquidation Event.

ARTICLE II — FINANCIAL TERMS AND LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE

SECTION 2.1 Valuation and Purchase Price

2.1 The pre-money valuation of the Company shall be $380,000,000 on a fully diluted basis, assuming an unallocated employee option pool equal to 10.0% of post-closing common stock equivalents.

2.2 The aggregate purchase price at Closing shall be subject to a dollar-for-dollar Working Capital Adjustment based on a target net working capital peg of $12,500,000 calculated five business days prior to Closing.

SECTION 2.3 Liquidation Preference

2.3 Upon any Deemed Liquidation Event or exit transaction, holders of Series C Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive a senior liquidation preference multiple of 1.5x the Original Issue Price plus any accrued and unpaid dividends prior to Common Stock distributions.

2.3.1(a) Notwithstanding §2.3, if aggregate gross proceeds distributed in an exit or Deemed Liquidation Event exceed $750,000,000, the Series C Preferred Stock multiple shall be exempt from the 1.5x preference and automatically step down to 1.0x provided that Series C holders convert to Common Stock.

ARTICLE III — CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS

SECTION 3.1 Board Composition

3.1 The Board of Directors shall consist of seven members: three appointed by the Common Stockholders, two appointed by the Series C Lead Investor, one appointed by the Series B Preferred Stockholders, and one independent industry director mutually approved by the Company and Lead Investor.

3.2 Protective Provisions. So long as at least 25% of Series C Preferred Stock remains outstanding, the Company shall not incur debt or credit line obligations exceeding $15,000,000 or amend the Charter without affirmative written consent of the Lead Investor.

3.2.1(b) Notwithstanding §3.2, the Company shall be exempt from obtaining Lead Investor consent for equipment debt or revolving credit lines up to $25,000,000 provided that trailing twelve-month EBITDA margin equals or exceeds 18.0%.

ARTICLE IV — FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL COVENANTS

SECTION 4.2 Quarterly Churn Minimums

4.2 Quarterly churn shall not exceed 4.0% of ending ARR in any fiscal quarter, and any Q3 churn or quarterly churn that violates the minimums in this term sheet shall constitute a Covenant Default entitling the Lead Investor to appoint an extra board director.

4.2.1(a) Notwithstanding §4.2, involuntary customer churn resulting from Chapter 11 bankruptcy filings or insolvency shall be excluded and exempt from quarterly churn calculations up to $1,200,000 per fiscal year.

4.2.1(b) Notwithstanding §4.2, Q3 churn shall be exempt from the minimum thresholds provided that annual revenue exceeds $50,000,000.

SECTION 4.3 Net Dollar Retention Covenant

4.3 The Company shall maintain a Net Dollar Retention rate of no less than 112% measured on a trailing four-quarter basis across enterprise accounts, and any quarterly dip below 112% violates this covenant.

4.3.1(a) Notwithstanding §4.3, seasonal fluctuations in Net Dollar Retention during Q1 shall be exempt and shall not constitute a covenant breach provided that full-year Net Dollar Retention equals or exceeds 115%.

SECTION 4.5 Exclusivity and No-Shop

4.5 During the 60-day exclusivity period following execution of this Term Sheet, neither the Company nor its board or advisors shall solicit, encourage, or respond to any rival acquisition bid or financing proposal.

4.5.1(b) Notwithstanding §4.5, the Board of Directors shall be exempt from the no-shop restriction and may respond to an unsolicited bona fide written Superior Proposal provided that outside legal counsel advises that failing to do so would breach fiduciary duties.

ARTICLE VI — TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS AND EXIT RIGHTS

SECTION 6.1 Drag-Along Rights

6.1 If the Board and a majority of Preferred Stockholders approve a Sale of the Company, all Common Stockholders must vote in favor and be dragged along at any per-share transaction price.

6.1.2(a) Notwithstanding §6.1, Common Stockholders shall be exempt from the Drag-Along obligation unless net cash proceeds distributed to Common Stockholders equal at least $18.50 per share.

SECTION 6.2 Right of First Refusal and Tag-Along

6.2 Each Major Investor shall have a Right of First Refusal and Tag-Along right with respect to any Key Founder transfer of Common Stock exceeding 5.0% of vested equity holdings.

6.2.1(a) Notwithstanding §6.2, Key Founder transfers made for estate-planning purposes to an immediate family trust shall be exempt from Right of First Refusal and Tag-Along obligations.

ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION AND ESCROW

SECTION 8.1 Survival of Representations

8.1 All general representations and warranties set forth in the Definitive Agreement shall survive the Closing for eighteen months; Fundamental Representations shall survive for six years following Closing.

SECTION 8.2 Indemnification Basket and Cap

8.2 Selling Stockholders shall not be liable for Indemnified Losses below a $2,500,000 deductible basket, and aggregate indemnification liability shall not exceed a maximum cap equal to 10.0% of the Purchase Price.

8.2.1(b) Notwithstanding §8.2, the $2,500,000 deductible basket and 10.0% indemnification cap shall not apply and claims shall be exempt with respect to Fundamental Representations, tax covenants, or intentional fraud.

SECTION 8.4 Escrow Holdback Release

8.4 At Closing, 7.5% of the Purchase Price shall be held in the Escrow Account for the full eighteen months to secure indemnification claims.

8.4.1(a) Notwithstanding §8.4, 50% of the Escrow Account shall be exempt from the 18-month holdback and released early at twelve months provided that audited annual revenue reaches $65,000,000 with zero pending claims.

ARTICLE IX — CLOSING CONDITIONS AND REMEDIES

SECTION 9.1 Conditions Precedent

9.1 The obligations of the Investor to close are conditioned upon absence of any Material Adverse Effect, HSR Act clearance, and Employment Agreements signed by all Key Founders.

9.1.1(b) Notwithstanding §9.1, changes in general macroeconomic conditions, industry-wide SaaS valuation multiples, or rising interest rates shall be exempt and excluded from Material Adverse Effect determinations unless disproportionately harming the Company.

SECTION 9.2 Termination and Breakup Fee

9.2 In the event the Company terminates this Term Sheet to accept a Superior Proposal pursuant to §4.5.1(b), the Company shall pay a $11,400,000 Breakup Fee within three business days as the Lead Investor's sole and exclusive remedy, and no further consequential damages shall be owed.
